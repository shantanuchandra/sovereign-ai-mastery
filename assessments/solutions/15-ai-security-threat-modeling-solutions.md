# Module 15 facilitator solutions

Facilitator assessment artifact; exclude from the public build. Educational rubric, not independent security approval. Distinct from the 20-point PM deliverable. Date: 28 November 2026.

## Q1. Assets, entry points and boundaries — 4 points

Asset: protected value such as note confidentiality or entitlement integrity. Entry point: where content/control enters. Trust boundary: authority/exposure changes. An untrusted retrieved string crossing into a privileged tool proposal is a boundary even inside one process. One point each; a physical location alone is not identity authority.

## Q2. Injection and consequences — 6 points

Two points: indirect external content can influence a proposal without becoming permission. Two: filter retrieval by current trusted principal/tenant/resource before context and recheck state. Two: independently enforce tool/schema/operation/resource/destination and exact-action approval as needed. A refusal or keyword detector alone earns no enforcement credit. A free-form response channel can still disclose data and needs separate controls.

## Q3. Artifact trust — 6 points

Two: matching hashes show consistency relative to an expected value, not who supplied it. Two: the expected identity must come from a separately controlled trusted source; attacker changing artifact and manifest defeats adjacent-only checks. Two: verify signature/attestation binding, issuer/root, validity, inputs/process and consumer policy, while retaining behavioral and rights review. The lab is no signed provenance implementation.

## Q4. Different claims — 4 points

One each: least privilege addresses permitted authority; premises placement addresses eligible location/operator/connectivity; a BOM inventories components and relationships; a test provides bounded evidence about a specified control/configuration. None alone implies the others or full security.

## Q5. Source interpretation — 8 points

Two: OWASP gives general recommendations, not a vendor/customer assessment. Two: one refusal has a narrow observed scope and cannot establish attack coverage. Two: realistic authorized downstream denial, identity, platform, model/output-channel and integrity validation can support particular control claims. Two: independently inspect all lifecycle locations/operators/network/update/recovery paths for the physical claim; conflicts remain blocked, missing evidence stays open. No real vendor incident is asserted.

## Q6. Decision and root cause — 12 points

Two: move from UNVERIFIED to observed FAILED action boundary and BLOCKED known off-site conflict; keep evidence states separate. Two: direct cause is unrestricted adapter executing a hostile proposal with broad authority. Two: systemic causes include treating content as permission, shared administrator privilege, absent independent invocation policy and incomplete diagnostics inventory. Two: contain export, revoke compromised credentials, preserve minimized evidence, identify affected records and assign application/security/operations/data owners. Two: implement narrow independent authorization and destination controls, verify artifact/identity paths and run authorized denial tests on a fixed revision with a specific retest date such as 30 November 2026. Two: later action tests passing support only that claim; external diagnostics retain BLOCKED deployment under unchanged BANK-A policy. Prompt patch and quality scores cannot waive it.

**Total 40 points.** Suggested educational threshold 32/40, with no claim that scoring closes hard conflicts, independent assessment, manual accessibility, real identity, production security or facility evidence gaps.
