## Q1. Three different questions (4 points)

Location describes where processing or artifacts exist. Territorial scope asks whether the activity falls under a specified instrument; establishment, individuals and activity facts matter. A named-site rule is an authorized enterprise requirement with an exact physical boundary and artifact inventory. Award one point per distinction and one for concrete evidence. Reject a country label treated as proof of the other two.

## Q2. Entity and activity map (4 points)

Request the legal entity and role, purpose and intended use, people/data classes, actual recipients and onward recipients, operator locations, retention, support and recovery. Route separately to qualified federal/free-zone reviewers. Award one point for entities/roles, one for activity facts, one for lifecycle recipients and one for open questions with ownership. Avoid assuming a single group-wide regime or declaring an exemption from a brand/address alone.

## Q3. Saudi support route (4 points)

Inspect the amended PDPL Art. 29 and transfer regulation Arts. 4–7 linked in the chapter. Establish actual bundle contents, recipient, purpose, onward processing, proposed mechanism and required review evidence. A heading on a contract proves none of those facts. Award one point for the correct source set, two for route-specific facts and one for explicitly pending legal interpretation. Do not reward a universal Saudi localization claim. Source: SDAIA official English displays inspected 2026-11-22, URLs in learner ledger.

## Q4. Negative tests and limits (4 points)

Propose synthetic timeout, licence renewal, update, support and restore tests; require no network path in the disconnected scenario and approved offline artifact custody. Inspect application configuration, gateway/DNS evidence, host interfaces, out-of-band management and physical/operator procedures. A JSON boolean or supplied passing observation is not proof of disconnection. Award two points for lifecycle coverage, one for observations and one for limits/owners.

## Q5. Real guidance, invented buyer (6 points)

The documented DIFC guidance distinguishes adequacy, conditional transfer safeguards/exceptions and authority-sharing questions; it does not certify the fictional buyer's plant-only architecture. A contract may be an input to a legal transfer review. It cannot move an off-site endpoint into PLANT-A or amend the signed site boundary. Keep the route blocked against that requirement, map preprocessing/support/backup, and request actual configurations, recipients, test observations and the authorized interpretation. A competent owner could consider an explicit requirement revision; silently weakening it is unacceptable.

Award two points for accurate, bounded reading of the named real source; two for the physical-site veto and retained unknowns; two for falsifiable evidence and authorized change handling. ## Q6. Step-by-step RCA (18 points)

1. **Observation and state (3):** UNVERIFIED becomes BLOCKED/HOLD after the reported prohibited attempt. Gateway denial does not establish confirmed export; distinguish application behavior, enforcement behavior and incident scope.
2. **Immediate cause (2):** Investigate support-upload configuration/SDK fallback and timeout handling; this is a hypothesis until artifacts confirm it.
3. **Systemic cause (2):** Requirements/procurement/test coverage omitted derivative support and recovery flows, and did not assign an evidence owner. Validate through actual versioned records rather than blaming an individual.
4. **Preservation/containment (2):** Preserve synthetic input, build/configuration, attempted route, gateway result, error and bundle schema; block release and disable prohibited paths pending review. Do not put real bundles in the public journal.
5. **Owners/remediation (3):** Platform owner removes or localizes diagnostics/fallback, procurement and legal review dependency/contract terms, operations validates support/update/restore, data owner retains boundary authority. Merely signing clauses or suppressing warnings does not fix routing.
6. **Retest (3):** Repeat normal, timeout, crash, licence, update and restore scenarios with an intentionally prohibited destination as a negative control. Correlate app and enforcement evidence. Physical air-gap review includes all interfaces and offline operations; no temporary remote-support exception.
7. **Bounded closure and residual risk (3):** Record exact tested version, reviewer, date, unresolved risks and scope. The evaluator can return REVIEW_REQUIRED only on complete recorded assertions; it grants no real approval. If eligible local/offline options cannot meet required licence, capacity or quality, state NOT FEASIBLE UNDER CURRENT CONSTRAINTS and propose authorized non-AI operations. Reopen on supplier, route, artifact or policy change.

Total 40 points. Suggested formative target: 30/40 plus no hard-boundary override. This is a course-authored learning target, not an accredited pass mark. Keep first attempts separate from coached remediation.
