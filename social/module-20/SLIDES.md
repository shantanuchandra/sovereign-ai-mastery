# Module 20 question-led carousel

HTML is the authoring source. Eight 4:5 portrait slides. Synthetic exercise, not production assurance.

## 1. What repeated job justifies a platform?

A model catalog counts features. A strategy identifies an eligible customer job and an outcome that shared services can improve.

Decision: Write a hypothesis that pilot observations could disprove.

Source/scope: Course product framework; fictional BANK-A.

## 2. Whose decision does onboarding require?

Developer, business owner, IT, security and AI engineer need different evidence. Requesting access does not grant approval authority.

Decision: Name requester, reviewer and operating owner.

Source/scope: Course stakeholder design; no enterprise approvals.

## 3. Can a score override a forbidden path?

For fictional BANK-A, external traces or remote support conflict with its physical-premises rule. Air gap also requires disconnected operation.

Decision: Apply hard eligibility gates before preference weights.

Source/scope: Illustrative contractual premise; no legal conclusion.

## 4. Does an API document enforce access?

OpenAPI describes an interface. OWASP API1 asks developers to check authorization on record-accessing functions. A schema alone proves neither.

Decision: Test the actual tenant and resource boundary.

Source/scope: OpenAPI 3.1.1 and OWASP API1:2023; checked 28 Nov 2026.

## 5. What happens when a client retries?

The synthetic fixture scopes replay keys to a tenant and binds operation plus content. Identical retry adds no event; changed reuse fails.

Decision: Define durability, concurrency and expiry before production.

Source/scope: RFC 9110 idempotency concept; fixture is not HTTP.

## 6. What does ACTIVE_FIXTURE establish?

Four ordered synthetic stages and four events show a bounded onboarding flow. Acceptance flags are declarations, not independent test evidence.

Decision: Preserve real identity and physical evidence as open gates.

Source/scope: Actual retained fixture observation; no production service.

## 7. Who disappears from your adoption metric?

A favorable completion ratio can hide paused or abandoned requests. Define the cohort, timing, exclusions and separate reason counts.

Decision: Show the full journey and the action on a missed target.

Source/scope: Course KPI design; targets and pilot dates illustrative.

## 8. Which dependency changes the next quarter?

Identity precedes self-service. Rights and evaluation precede catalog growth. Recovery precedes promises that depend on restoration.

Decision: Roadmap twelve months with conditional gates and owners.

Source/scope: Educational PRD; independent and manual reviews pending.
