# Module 05 — eight decision questions

Educational draft; all deployment examples fictional. Source legal discussion and exact primary-source ledger are in the chapter.

## 1. Which decision should legal review answer?

Give reviewers the activity, purpose, entities, recipients and actual routes. A country name alone does not describe a processing operation.

Prepare a route-specific question and name its owner.

## 2. Does one group mean one privacy regime?

In a fictional group, mainland UAE, DIFC and ADGM activities require separate scope questions. Do not infer a group-wide answer from a shared brand.

Map the entity and activity before selecting instruments.

## 3. Can a transfer clause move a server into your factory?

A hypothetical PLANT-A contract keeps data and derivatives on the physical site. A domestic off-site endpoint still contradicts that requirement.

Retain the hard boundary until an authorized change.

## 4. Where does the speech go before inference?

Local final inference can hide remote transcription, OCR, telemetry or support handling. Include intermediate artifacts and failure paths.

Inventory preprocessing, logs, support and backups.

## 5. Can a physical air gap call home for a licence?

The fictional disconnected terminal prohibits every inbound and outbound network path. Activation, updates, support and restore must support approved offline operation.

Validate offline rights, artifacts and operating procedures.

## 6. What does a blocked upload actually prove?

A gateway denial shows an attempted route was blocked in that test. It does not prove a confirmed disclosure or an acceptable application design.

Preserve evidence, remove the route and retest adverse paths.

## 7. Does a complete register mean compliant?

The synthetic lab checks missing evidence and reported conflicts. Even complete invented review records produce REVIEW_REQUIRED, never a deployment approval.

Ask people to review scope, source interpretation and actual evidence.

## 8. What belongs in the executive decision?

Connect each activity to a reviewer question, boundary, control, negative test, evidence owner and reapproval trigger. Record uncertainty explicitly.

Defend a bounded decision with traceable evidence.
