## 1. Which copies survive when a manual is retired?

The fictional PLANT-A assistant creates source rows, chunks, lexical vectors and snapshots. Removing the first row is only one lifecycle action.

Map every derivative to its source and retirement rule.

Source/scope: Course-authored synthetic factory scenario.

## 2. Who owns the handling rule after transformation?

A steward maintains classification and lifecycle decisions. Platform operators implement them. A transformed file still needs an accountable owner, purpose and approved destination.

A label alone does not enforce a policy.

Source/scope: Course-authored ownership model.

## 3. Does a vector make restricted text anonymous?

This lab stores word-count dictionaries. The words remain visible. A source hash or identifier can also preserve linkage; a new format does not justify a weaker boundary.

Inspect the representation before changing its handling.

Source/scope: Lexical toy model; no anonymity guarantee.

## 4. When should tenant and expiry checks happen?

The library filters tenant scope and expiry before ranking. Its caller supplies both scope and the fixture clock; it does not authenticate a person or implement a trusted service clock.

Derive production scope and time from trusted services.

Source/scope: Synthetic library; no production authorization claim.

## 5. What can an old SQLite snapshot bring back?

SQLite documents the Backup API as creating a snapshot. In our hypothetical application, a pre-retirement copy can contain old data. A separate ledger reapplies retirement after restore.

Test recovery against current retirement decisions.

Source/scope: SQLite Backup API, section 1; hypothetical application.

## 6. Can hiding an excerpt repair failed retirement?

A controlled test retrieves an expired manual after restore. Hiding it in the interface leaves the lifecycle failure. Renaming vectors anonymous does not repair their lineage or access rules.

Contain retrieval, fix eligibility and recovery, then retest.

Source/scope: Course-authored root-cause exercise.

## 7. Does local inference excuse remote preprocessing?

Under the fictional factory-only rule, off-site OCR is a conflict even when final inference is local. Source, extracted text, prompts, diagnostics, backups and support paths need explicit boundaries.

Keep physical location and connectivity as separate gates.

Source/scope: Enterprise scenario assumption, not a legal conclusion.

## 8. What evidence belongs in the data-control specification?

Record purpose, owner, tenant scope, lineage, approved routes, expiry, retirement, backup inventory and restore tests. Unknown copies, legal holds and physical erasure need separate evidence.

Make a bounded recommendation with owners and review dates.
