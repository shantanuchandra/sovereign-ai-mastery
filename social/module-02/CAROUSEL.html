04 / THE CLASSES

Does private cloud mean air-gapped AI?

No. A private link, domestic endpoint, vendor support tunnel or internet allowlist is still a network path. A physically air-gapped requirement rejects inbound and outbound connectivity.

DECISION TEST

Use signed offline artifacts, controlled removable media, local validation and a tested rollback.
