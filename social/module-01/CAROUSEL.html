COURSE-AUTHORED FRAMEWORK03 / 08
QUESTION 03THE TRAP

Does “hosted locally” answer who has control?

Not necessarily. Consider support access, diagnostic logs, encryption keys, failover routes and vendor exit.

Local hosting can support territorial control while other dependencies remain.

REGION ≠ COMPLETE SOVEREIGNTY04 / 08
QUESTION 04THE ENTERPRISE

Can a company build sovereign AI?

Yes—by defining and demonstrating control over specific workloads. That does not mean owning every chip, model or software dependency.

Sovereignty is a bounded capability, not an ownership myth.

ENTERPRISE + WORKLOAD SCOPE05 / 08
QUESTION 05THE PROOF

What would prove a sovereignty claim wrong?

Write a claim that can fail. Map it to a control, run a test, retain dated evidence, and record exceptions.

Claim → Control → Test → Evidence → Decision
Unknown evidence is not a pass.
