# Which deployment can your requirement actually admit?

Does private cloud mean inside your building? What happens to support bundles, IaC state and backup copies when inference stays local? Can one site satisfy a requirement to survive losing all of itself?

Module 10 compares three fictional bank deployments and replays a supplied checkpoint/failure/restore timeline. A higher preference score cannot clear a forbidden path. Recovery time and recoverable data age need separate acceptance criteria.

The standard-library lab runs locally without a cloud account. Its times, scores and costs are invented; it measures no provider or real recovery.
