# Module 03: Eight questions before buying sovereign AI

> Editorial source for `CAROUSEL.html`. All named enterprise events and policies are fictional, not statements of UAE law or vendor behavior. Do not imply a deployment has been certified.

## Slide 1: Which sovereignty requirement actually changes the architecture?

Start with an observable business failure. A signed physical-site restriction is a hard gate; a preference for vendor independence is an investment objective.

**Decision evidence:** Decision: identify the authority, asset boundary, prohibited action and test before comparing vendors.

## Slide 2: Can a UAE-hosted service satisfy a named-factory rule?

Not merely because the service is in the UAE. The fictional FACTORY-A contract prohibits restricted prompts, embeddings, outputs, telemetry and backups from leaving the named premises.

**Decision evidence:** Rejected: remote inference, even within the same country. Evidence: site-scoped packet, DNS, proxy and backup traces.

## Slide 3: What if the cloud is cheaper and more accurate?

Economics and model quality rank only candidates that pass mandatory constraints. A weighted score must never override a hard placement or connectivity gate.

**Decision evidence:** Choose an eligible local architecture or record NOT FEASIBLE UNDER CURRENT CONSTRAINTS.

## Slide 4: Who can approve a sovereignty exception?

A PM does not invent legal authority. Business, security, privacy, legal, architecture, operations and procurement have different evidence and decision rights.

**Decision evidence:** Document the named approver, signed exception scope, expiry, and prohibited override conditions.

## Slide 5: What happens when the local gateway times out?

A fictional gateway attempts remote fallback. The firewall denies egress, but the application still violated its premises-only routing policy.

**Decision evidence:** Firewall egress PASS is not application policy PASS. Block release; remove remote fallback and retest degraded mode.

## Slide 6: Can an air-gapped system call a licence server?

No. The fictional OT-03 policy prohibits inbound and outbound network paths, including support, updates, licence validation and diagnostics.

**Decision evidence:** Require offline licensing, signed offline artifact transfer, local restore and a physical network-path inspection.

## Slide 7: Which evidence should expire after a change?

A new model, gateway rule, subprocessor, log sink, backup path or support route can invalidate the prior approval.

**Decision evidence:** Link each requirement to owner, test, evidence timestamp, version and change-triggered reapproval.

## Slide 8: What would you put in the board decision?

Present eligible options, rejected alternatives, six measurable outcomes, accountable owners, unresolved tests and a 30/60/90-day roadmap.

**Decision evidence:** Approve a bounded synthetic pilot only after negative tests and signed reviews. Otherwise: HOLD.
