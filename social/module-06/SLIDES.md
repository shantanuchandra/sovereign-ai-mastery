# Module 06: eight governance questions

Educational draft. Fictional examples; independent assessment pending.

## Who can stop an unsafe release?
A fictional factory assistant needs a named operator, incident contact and usable manual fallback. A committee calendar cannot contain an unsafe answer.

Exercise the stop path before the pilot.

Source/scope: Fictional PLANT-A scenario.

## Can 1.58% overall error hide a 30% failure?
Synthetic English: 98 errors in 9,800 queries. Arabic: 60 in 200. The overall average hides a much worse language cohort.

Retain denominators and test the affected tasks.

Source/scope: Course-authored synthetic counts.

## Does a standard replace a control test?
NIST AI RMF says: “Actions do not constitute a checklist”. Our course application connects each risk to a control, owner, observation and decision.

Ask what the exact build demonstrated.

Source/scope: NIST AI RMF 1.0, Core introduction.

## Which build does the evidence describe?
A v2 oversight record does not validate v3 automatically. The retrieval index, source corpus, permissions or prompt can change behavior.

Bind the test, date and outcome to release identity.

Source/scope: Course-authored evidence model.

## Can a risk signature move the boundary?
PLANT-A requires protected data and derivatives to remain in its physical factory. A diagnostic copy at a domestic vendor campus conflicts with that unchanged requirement.

Block the route and assign a repair and retest.

Source/scope: Fictional premises-only requirement.

## Does an offline PDF prove an offline pipeline?
Microsoft documents offline sharing of a dashboard scorecard. That describes the exported document; it does not establish where evaluation, telemetry or activation run.

Inspect every data and control path.

Source/scope: Microsoft Learn, Azure ML API v2 dashboard docs.

## Can your operator challenge the answer?
Test whether operators recognize an unsupported procedure, stop the action and reach an approved fallback under realistic time pressure.

Observe intervention rather than counting supervisors.

Source/scope: Course-authored oversight exercise.

## What would reopen the decision?
A changed use, failed observation, expired evidence or incident should trigger a fresh review. Favorable synthetic records still require human judgment.

Record scope, owner, residual uncertainty and review date.
