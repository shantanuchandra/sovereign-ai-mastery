# Who can stop your AI release?

If one language has a 30% error rate while the overall rate is 1.58%, which number reaches the release meeting?

Module 06 uses a fictional Arabic-English factory assistant to connect governance to decisions:

- Who owns the intended use and excluded actions?
- Does the evidence describe this exact build?
- Can an operator detect, stop and escalate an unsafe answer?
- Do diagnostics, support and recovery respect the physical factory boundary?
- Which incident or change reopens approval?

The local lab calculates subgroup errors, checks declared control evidence and rejects known boundary conflicts. Its synthetic fixtures never certify a real deployment.

The eight-slide carousel asks one decision question per page. The chapter distinguishes NIST guidance, public ISO management-system descriptions and observations from a specific control test. Independent assessment and manual accessibility review remain pending.
