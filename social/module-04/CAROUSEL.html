Sovereign AI Mastery / Decision 01

What does a national AI strategy prove?

It signals public priorities. It does not certify a vendor, grant a data-transfer exemption, or prove a workload is sovereign.

Decision: separate policy direction from buyer-specific evidence.

Sovereign AI Mastery / Decision 02

Can one GCC deployment policy cover every country?

No. UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Oman, Bahrain and Kuwait have distinct authorities, sector rules and operating conditions. This comparison is a research starting point, not legal advice.

Decision: record the jurisdiction, applicable entity and reviewing authority per workload.

Does UAE residency satisfy a named-factory contract?

Not automatically. In a fictional industrial deployment, prompts, embeddings, speech, telemetry and backups must remain inside FACTORY-A, not merely inside the UAE.

Decision: reject off-premises processing until a competent authority changes the requirement.

Can a physical air gap include remote vendor support?

No. The fictional OT-47 terminal prohibits every inbound and outbound network path, including licence checks, updates, support and management.

Decision: require offline licensing, offline artifact handling and physical inspection.

Sovereign AI Mastery / Decision 05

What if Arabic text scores well but speech fails?

A model's text benchmark does not validate speech recognition, dialects, OCR, retrieval or unsafe fallback paths. Test each component and each language slice.

Decision: require component-level accuracy, safety and destination evidence.

Sovereign AI Mastery / Decision 06

What happens when speech falls back to a cloud endpoint?

In the fictional PLANT-47 exercise, a gateway blocks the request. The firewall works, but the application still attempted a prohibited route.

Decision: HOLD release, remove fallback, preserve logs and retest timeout and recovery.

Sovereign AI Mastery / Decision 07

Which infrastructure dependency could reverse the decision?

Compute supply, energy, licences, support access, model weights and backup destinations can defeat an otherwise attractive architecture.

Decision: compare eligible local, managed and non-AI alternatives before ranking cost.

Sovereign AI Mastery / Decision 08

What should the executive approval contain?

A dated source ledger, hard-gate eligibility, named owners, test evidence, unresolved risks and a reapproval trigger for any changed route or supplier.

Decision: approve only a bounded pilot after evidence; otherwise record HOLD or NOT FEASIBLE.
