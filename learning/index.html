EXPERIMENT 001 / THE DECISION ENGINE

Design the system.
Defend the choice.

An enterprise architecture generator and adaptive tutor in one learning loop. Choose from real-world categories, including strict on-premises and air-gapped workloads. Inspect architectural trade-offs, practice decisions, then change an assumption to see where the proposal changes.

THE METHODRequirements → architecture → evidence
THE OUTPUTBlueprint + tested reasoning
THE RULENever confuse a claim with proof
This is an educational rule engine, not an AI chat model. Recommendations are provisional and course-authored. They do not validate vendors, certify sovereignty, judge regulatory applicability, or inspect actual infrastructure. All sample scenarios are fictional. A premises-only answer forbids sending protected data or derivatives to external services. Inputs are stored only in your browser until you export them.

03 · TERRITORIAL
Include inference, embeddings, logs, backups, diagnostics and disaster recovery. Premises means a physical site, not a cloud region.
04 · CONNECTIVITY
Air-gapped means no network. Premises-only cannot silently allow vendor telemetry or external inference.
05 · OPERATIONAL
Includes break-glass and support paths.
06 · TECHNOLOGICAL
Portability requires a trial, not a slogan.
07 · CAPACITY
A local M4 Pro is a lab, not a production SLA.
08 · EVIDENCE
Having a report does not mean it passed.
09 · JURISDICTION
Qualified counsel determines applicability.
10 · RELEASE
Risk appetite and assurance change by stage.
Selections update the blueprint immediately.
