## L05-06 Saudi transfer regulation: bounded primary instrument check

- **Issuer:** Saudi Data & Artificial Intelligence Authority (SDAIA). **Document:** *Regulation on Personal Data Transfer Outside the Kingdom*, English issuer-hosted text, [SDAIA PDF](https://sdaia.gov.sa/en/SDAIA/about/Documents/ImplementingRegulation.pdf) and [SDAIA regulatory knowledge-center entry](https://dgp.sdaia.gov.sa/wps/portal/pdp/knowledgecenter/), inspected 2026-11-23. The English PDF identifies the PDPL issued by Royal Decree **M/19** and amended by **M/148** in its definitions. This establishes the amendment referenced by the PDF, **not** that no later amendments exist or that English is the authoritative version.
- **Article 2(1)-(3)** of the transfer regulation permits conditional transfers, requires minimum-necessary data, and requires preserving data-subject protection. Article 2(2) expressly discusses a **data map** connecting each transferred data item to its purpose. **Article 4(1)-(2)** names safeguards including standard contractual clauses, binding common rules and certification for defined exemption cases. The text expressly ties exemptions to conditions in **PDPL Article 29(2)(b)-(c)**; it does **not** create an unconditional exemption for any Saudi-related workload.
- **Source separation:** Article 29 of the PDPL itself has **not** been independently read from the operative consolidated Arabic law in this check. The SDAIA regulation's reference to Article 29 is not a substitute for reading it. Nor have Articles 6-7 of the transfer regulation or its amendment history been exhaustively verified.
- **Disposition:** **PARTIAL VERIFIED EXCERPTS / HOLD**. Next obtain current Arabic PDPL and transfer-regulation editions, verify Article 29 and regulation Articles 4, 6, 7, amendments and commencement; qualified Saudi counsel to assess the fictional activity and exemptions.
