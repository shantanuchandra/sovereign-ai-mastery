## L05-01: UAE federal, article-level text verified (partial)

- **Issuer/document:** UAE Legislation portal, *Federal Decree by Law No. (45) of 2021 Concerning the Protection of Personal Data*, English download, [issuer PDF](https://www.uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/1972/download), checked **2026-11-23**. The downloaded English text identifies the 2021 instrument; **consolidation and subsequent amendment status not independently established**.
- **Pinpoint:** Article 2(1)(b) and (c), PDF pages 5-6: the text includes controllers/processors resident in the UAE processing data subjects inside/outside the UAE, and certain controllers/processors outside the UAE processing data subjects inside it. Short quote from Article 2(1)(c): “Each Controller or Processor residing outside the State”.
- **Pinpoint:** Article 2(2)(e), (f), (g), PDF pages 5-6: express exclusions include covered personal health data, banking/credit data with specific legislation, and qualifying special-law free-zone establishments. Short quote from Article 2(2)(e): “Personal Health Data that has legislation regulating its protection and processing.”
- **Chapter comparison:** §5.1 already correctly refuses a simple location-based scope conclusion and asks about health, banking and free-zone exceptions. **No chapter rewrite justified** by this check. Counsel must establish whether a particular entity/activity falls within each exclusion and check applicable sector instruments and implementing measures.
- **Unverified:** Current Arabic authoritative text, amendments/consolidation, operative executive regulations, specific transfer conditions, actual GulfCare applicability. **Decision: HOLD**.

## L05-02: DIFC consolidated transfer provisions verified (partial)

- **Issuer/document:** DIFC legal database's [issuer-hosted Data Protection Law PDF](https://assets.difc.com/v1/media/edge/images/dubaiintern0078-difcexperie96c5-production-3253/media/project/difcexperiences/difc/difcwebsite/documents/laws--regulations/data-protection-law.pdf), *DIFC Law No. 5 of 2020, Consolidated Version (July 2025)*, front page explicitly lists amendment **Law No. 1 of 2025** and **Law No. 2 of 2022**; checked **2026-11-23**. [Issuer legal index](https://www.difc.com/business/laws-and-regulations/legal-database/difc-laws/data-protection-law-difc-law-no-5-2020).
- **Pinpoint:** Article 26(1)(a)-(b), printed page 17: transfers to third countries/international organisations require an adequate-protection route or a route under Article 27. Short quote: “or it takes place in accordance with Article 27.”
- **Pinpoint:** Article 27(1)-(2), printed pages 17-18: non-adequate transfers involve specified safeguards, derogations or limited circumstances, with Article 27(2)(c) listing Commissioner-adopted standard clauses. Short quote: “standard data protection clauses as adopted by the Commissioner”.
- **Pinpoint:** Article 27(4)-(5), printed pages 18-19: exceptional nonrepetitive limited transfers have cumulative requirements, with Commissioner and subject notice conditions. Do not present these as a general exemption.
- **Important amendment nuance:** DIFC's [16 July 2025 issuer announcement](https://www.difc.com/whats-on/news/difc-announces-enactment-of-amendments-to-select-difc-legislation-through-difc-law-amendment-law) says the 2025 amendment also changes scope and **Article 28** government data sharing. Reviewing Articles 26-27 alone does not cover all current data-sharing routes. The chapter's §5.2 focuses on transfer review and does not assert that Article 28 is unchanged.
- **Chapter comparison:** The chapter's distinction between transfer assessment and legal permission is supported; retain counsel gate. No claim of enterprise approval follows from a model clause or an adequacy entry.
- **Unverified:** Whether this is the latest issuer consolidation after July 2025, any subsequent enactments, exact GulfCare Article 6 scope, applicable regulations, transfer destination status, signed contractual safeguards, and legal applicability. **Decision: HOLD**.
