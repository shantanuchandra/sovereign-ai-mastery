# Saudi transfer regulation: issuer-hosted English version collision (draft-only)

**Inspected:** 2026-11-23. **Reviewer:** GPT-6 author-side source check, not independent legal review. **Decision:** HOLD; legal applicability NEEDS_COUNSEL.

## Observed primary-source discrepancy

Both pages are hosted by the Saudi Data & AI Authority (SDAIA) and titled *Regulation on Personal Data Transfer Outside the Kingdom*. Their English article numbering and subjects differ:

| Source displayed 2026-11-23 | Article 6 | Article 7 | Risk assessment article |
|---|---|---|---|
| [SDAIA /RegulationonPersonalDataTransferOutsidetheKingdom/](https://dgp.sdaia.gov.sa/wps/portal/pdp/knowledgecenter/details/RegulationonPersonalDataTransferOutsidetheKingdom/) | **Revocation of Exemption** | **Risk Assessment of Transferring or Disclosing Personal Data to a Party Outside the Kingdom** | 7 |
| [SDAIA /PDPL2/](https://dgp.sdaia.gov.sa/wps/portal/pdp/knowledgecenter/details/PDPL2/) | **Cases where the appropriate safeguards for Transfer of Personal Data outside the Kingdom are not required** | **Cases where Exemptions are not Granted** | 8 |

The first page's Article 6(1) addresses loss of an exemption when safeguards fail or are judged inadequate; Article 7(1) describes specified risk-assessment triggers. The second page's Article 6 lists certain exceptions when safeguards are unavailable; Article 7(1) lists cessation triggers and Article 8(1) sets risk-assessment triggers. This is a **substantive version collision**, not merely a different translation of one heading. The second page's Article 1 explicitly references Royal Decree M/19 and amendment M/148, but this does **not** independently establish which displayed English page is controlling today.
