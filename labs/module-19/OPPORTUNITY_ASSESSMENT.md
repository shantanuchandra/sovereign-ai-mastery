# Market opportunity assessment and prioritized use-case backlog

This is a distinct PM deliverable, scored out of 20 separately from the 40-point knowledge assessment. Use synthetic cases only. Course-derived decision framework; not a market valuation or assurance report.

## Reviewable assessment template

**Decision owner / date / horizon:** [role, YYYY-MM-DD, next decision date]

**Decision requested:** [bounded research, redesign, stop, or pilot investigation]

**Hypothesis:** For [user and situation], [job] should improve [outcome] versus [current alternative], while preserving [quality and control conditions]. Reject if [observed falsification condition].

**Buying center:** [user, process owner, economic buyer, technical/control approver, privacy/legal reviewer, procurement, operator; decision right and unresolved disagreement for each]

| Evidence ID | Observation or synthetic statement | Source, owner, checked date | State and limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| E-01 | [separate verbatim note and interpretation] | [traceable synthetic reference] | [observed/unknown/contradicted; no real buyer claimed] |

**Operational segments:** [job + buyer route + premises/approved-managed/disconnected boundary]. Explain what changes in support, updates, recovery and offer packaging.

| Opportunity | Gate state and reason | Desirability and assumptions | Necessity and next evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| FIN/HEALTH/TEL/GOV/IND | [do not rank conflicts/gaps] | [weights, or unknown] | [required/preferred/not-established; owner/date] |

**Alternative comparison:** [existing search, process change/manual review, approved managed tool, private RAG, postponement; incremental outcome, integration/operations burden, unresolved control evidence]

**Economic assumption:** [baseline units × proposed change × adoption, less verification/rework/operating effort; invented values labeled; no released-time-to-cash shortcut]

**Sensitivity:** [one numeric change and one boundary change; show different decision mechanisms]

**Next experiment:** [question that can reverse decision; allowed data; method/population; quality/control guardrails; cost/time boundary; owner; dated retest; stop criteria]

**Capstone handoff:** [Workload, BuyerEvidence, OpportunityHypothesis, DiscoveryDecision; unresolved constraints and owners]

## Rubric: 20 points

| Criterion | Points | Full-credit evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Buyer/job and decision rights | 4 | Concrete workflow, alternative, economic buyer/operator/control approver and disagreements |
| Evidence integrity | 4 | Traceable sources, dates, owners, synthetic labeling; unknown and contradiction retained |
| Eligibility and segmentation | 4 | Premises/airgap conflicts block before score; operating segments change packaging and alternative |
| Economics and prioritization | 4 | Reproducible assumptions/weights, separate necessity, numeric and boundary sensitivity |
| Next experiment and handoff | 4 | Falsification, guardrails, owner/date, stop/retest, bounded capstone decision |

Award 2–3 for partially complete but decision-useful work; 1 for assertions without traceability; 0 for absent or misleading work. Any invented real interview, fabricated market statistic, score overriding conflict or “compliant” verdict requires correction regardless of points. Facilitator should document required revisions and keep approval pending; the rubric is educational.
