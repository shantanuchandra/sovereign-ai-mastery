## Evidence checklist

- Licence reviewer, entity, intended use, modification/distribution scope, full terms, notices and disposition.
- Model, tokenizer, template, configuration, adapters, quantization, runtime/dependencies, revisions and trusted artifact hashes.
- Task dataset source/rights, en/ar/domain slices, raw outputs, passed/total denominators, criteria and qualified review.
- Load/memory/error evidence on the candidate build; runtime/deployment boundaries and all protected paths.
- Cold restart and exit: rebuild from approved artifacts without a hub call; repeat task tests; check logs, index, gateway and fallback.
- Costs: fixed operations, variable successful-request cost, utilization, staffing and sensitivity. Clearly label estimates.
- Decision state, blocked conflicts, gaps, accountable owners, evidence links, dates and rollback criteria.
