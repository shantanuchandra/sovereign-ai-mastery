## Dependency and concentration inventory

List model/tokenizer/runtime/license, source/index/embedding configuration, prompts, identity/permissions, keys, registry, telemetry, backups, support, activation, updates, failover and removable media. Include OCR/speech and protected derivatives. Record immutable references, custodians, export formats, recovery ordering, common providers/facilities and trusted verification source. Two logos do not establish independent failure domains.

## Restore, migration and substitute acceptance

Specify backup identity, schema, integrity/provenance trust, encrypted-key recovery, current retirement/revocation overlay and authorization before exposure. Preserve current decisions independently from historical data. Define whether retained archive content may be restored for investigation and how it is excluded from active serving; obtain qualified policy review.

Define exported and omitted fields, tenant/permission/lineage equality, schema/runtime compatibility, rights and source trust. PostgreSQL dump restoration can execute source-superuser-selected code; inspect source trust and version/global-object/extension needs before choosing that real mechanism. The fixture uses SQLite parameterized synthetic inserts and supplies no PostgreSQL evidence.

Set matched independent quality, Arabic/domain, grounding, refusal, tool, capacity and boundary criteria for a real substitute. Record cutover, rollback/roll-forward, unknown tool-outcome reconciliation and source retention. No production objective follows from the fixture's two equal migrated rows.

## Incident and exit authority

Name incident commander, recovery operator, security/identity owner, data/retirement owner, product acceptance owner, procurement/legal owner and communication lead. Specify emergency-change authority, required exact references, containment, evidence minimization and post-change review. Do not treat emergency speed as authority to violate BANK-A's unchanged premises rule.

Set exit triggers, notice/export access, transition assistance, deletion evidence, unresolved rights and contract questions. Budget overlap, storage, spare capacity, index rebuilding, operators and evaluation. Separate measured units, assumptions and verified quotes; no prices are supplied by this exercise.

## Assurance register and scoring

Retain requirement → claim → control → test → actual result → exception → accountable owner → dated retest, with version, environment, reviewer identity, scope and expiry. Attach the actual lab observation as narrow synthetic evidence. Keep independent security/assessment/manual accessibility, actual physical boundary and production recovery as NOT COMPLETED until performed.

Award 0–4 each for business objectives/continuity; complete dependencies/concentration; restore/current authorization and migration/substitution acceptance; incident/exit authority and economics; dated assurance/evidence/limitations. Total 20. Explain awarded points; the score cannot compensate for a hard conflict.

Decision options: BLOCKED for known conflict; UNVERIFIED for missing required evidence; a bounded conditional review recommendation for internally consistent synthetic declarations and passed scoped checks. DECLARED_ONLY is never production approval. Set a concrete owner-specific retest date, for example 30 November 2026, and preserve Module 04 HOLD.
