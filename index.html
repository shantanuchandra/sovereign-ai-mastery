EDITOR'S NOTE / 001

Most discussions about sovereign AI begin with a map. Where is the data? That is only one of several necessary questions. The harder questions concern who can change a model, who can operate the infrastructure, whose laws apply, and what happens when a vendor relationship ends.

This field guide is built for product leaders who must answer those questions in architecture reviews, boardrooms, and actual deployments. Not by borrowing certainty from a vendor brochure, but by tracing each claim to a control and to evidence.
