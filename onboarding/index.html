        THE STUDIO / 001

        A question is a design constraint.

        The point is not to score sovereignty. It is to learn which requirement changes architecture and which claim still needs evidence.

        1. 1
          State the workloadIdentify its data and business objective.
        2. 2
          Choose a control boundaryLocation, operations, portability, and jurisdiction.
        3. 3
          Test the recommendationChange one choice and inspect the consequences.
        4. 4
          Write down the evidence gapA credible claim can fail a test.
        Why doesn't it show a 92% sovereignty score?

        A single percentage can disguise critical failures. An untested operational boundary cannot be offset by excellent residency evidence.
