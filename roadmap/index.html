Adoption and value

One result earns the next user.

Peer-led growth starts when Colleague A shares a verified missing-documents checklist and Colleague B independently uses Jean to verify a checklist on a case they can access. Colleague B’s first verified run shows that a teammate can drive adoption. Their next verified run on a separate work cycle shows repeat use.

  1. 01

    Colleague A verifies the checklist

    Colleague A checks Jean’s citations and records staff time, rework and operating cost against the manual baseline.

  2. 02

    Colleague A shares with Colleague B

    Colleague A sends an in-tenant link if their role and case permissions allow sharing. Jean checks Colleague B’s current access when they open it.

  3. 03

    Colleague B runs a case independently

    After an authorised open, Colleague B starts a fresh run. Jean checks Colleague B’s workflow and evidence permissions for that case.

  4. 04

    Colleague B returns on the next work cycle

    Track whether Colleague B completes another verified run and whether the time and operating-cost savings persist.

Day-90 decision

When to invest in a second deployment

A second deployment requires accepted Recovery and Security evidence. If either gate remains open, record the owner and retest date and hold expansion.

Repeat use, verified savings and peer-led growth

Agree on paid continuation or a funded next step with the sponsor. Test the same workflow with a second willing customer and measure the deployment effort again.

Verified savings with limited peer-led growth

Watch where recipients drop off between the shared result and their own first run. Improve discovery, access or reuse before expanding the workflow catalogue.

Repeat use without verified savings

Inspect review, corrections and support effort. Narrow or revise the workflow and repeat the baseline comparison before investing in acquisition.

Release evidence and questions for kickoff

Decisions to make at kickoff

  • Confirm the design partner, workflow sponsor, and customer IT and Security approvers.
  • Allocate capacity for one read-only pilot across the ten shared developers.
  • Agree on the first source, the permitted deployment profile, and who can narrow scope when a dependency slips.

Release and impact evidence

  • Go when acceptance passes, IT completes rollback within 30 minutes and Security signs off on all mandatory checks and audit evidence.
  • Go with reduced scope when the sponsor accepts the remaining value and the scoped release passes all mandatory tests.
  • Hold on failed mandatory checks or pending approval. Record the owner and decision date.
  • Open the customer AI impact assessment in discovery. Update it at release and after live use, including rework, incidents, sample size and the observation window.

Questions to resolve by day 14

  • Which customer has a sponsor, an approved source and recurring work we can observe?
  • What savings would change the team’s default way of working?
  • Can recipients open a shared result and run a new case under their own permissions?
  • How much capacity is protected, and who can make a scope decision when a dependency slips?
