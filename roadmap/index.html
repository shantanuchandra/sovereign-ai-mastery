01 / ADOPTION AND VALUE

One result earns the next user.

A reviewer gets a cited checklist for a recurring document review, verifies it, and shares it with a colleague who has access. The colleague opens the evidence and reuses the task on their own case. That second verified run is the first sign that value can travel without the product team.

  1. 01

    Get a verified result

    Run the task, check the evidence, and record time and effort against the manual baseline.

  2. 02

    Share it with a colleague

    Share a link inside the customer’s approved environment. Check the recipient’s access when they open it.

  3. 03

    Reuse the workflow

    Start a fresh run from the shared result using the recipient’s own permissions and inputs.

  4. 04

    Return when the job recurs

    Measure independent completion and whether the savings persist. Use that evidence in the sponsor’s continuation discussion.

A / PRIMARY · VALUE-BACKED ADOPTION

Repeat users with verified savings

Report the share and count of eligible pilot users who complete a verified task on at least two distinct work cycles while meeting the agreed savings bar. An eligible user has access and at least two real opportunities in the observation window. Agree on the target share and minimum observation window by day 28.

I / WORKFLOW IMPACT

Time and resources saved

Compare like-for-like manual and assisted cases. Include citation review, correction, fallback and operator effort. Track median active minutes, rework and cost per verified result. Illustrative target: 30% less active time with no increase in material rework.

G / GOVERNANCE AND QUALITY

A release the customer can trust

Illustrative acceptance: 18 of 20 workflow cases pass, including valid abstentions. Every mandatory access, routing and policy case must pass. Customer IT demonstrates recovery and Security verifies the audit evidence.

R / PRODUCT REPEATABILITY

A deployment a second operator can run

Track critical steps reproduced from versioned instructions, operator hours and undocumented interventions. Freeze the step list and thresholds with the pilot owner by day 28.

Track the loop: verified result → share → authorised open → independent reuse → repeat use. Report counts and conversion between each step, using approved metadata. Track the sponsor’s willingness to fund continuation as a downstream commercial signal.

05 / DAY-90 DECISION

What would justify moving toward 1 → 10?

Repeat use + savings + peer reuse

Agree on paid continuation or a funded next step with the sponsor. Test the same workflow with a second willing customer and measure the deployment effort again.

Value without peer reuse

Watch where recipients drop off between the shared result and their own first run. Improve discovery, access or reuse before expanding the workflow catalogue.

Repeat use without verified savings

Inspect review, corrections and support effort. Narrow or revise the workflow and repeat the baseline comparison before investing in acquisition.

Service blueprint, release gates and kickoff questions

Service blueprint

  1. The user asks, checks citations and shares the result.
  2. The colleague opens permitted evidence and runs a new case.
  3. Behind the scenes, Jean retrieves current evidence, Simon routes inference, and the Gateway applies policy and records the decision.
  4. IT rehearses recovery. Product compares outcomes and operating effort against baseline.

Release and impact evidence

  • Go when workflow, control and recovery evidence meets the agreed bar.
  • Go with reduced scope when the sponsor accepts the remaining value and the scoped release passes all mandatory tests.
  • Hold on failed mandatory checks or pending approval. Record the owner and decision date.
  • Open the customer AI impact assessment in discovery. Update it at release and after live use, including rework, incidents, sample size and the observation window.

Questions to resolve by day 14

  • Which customer has a sponsor, an approved source and recurring work we can observe?
  • What savings would change the team’s default way of working?
  • Can recipients open a shared result and run a new case under their own permissions?
  • How much capacity is protected, and who can make a scope decision when a dependency slips?
